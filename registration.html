Payment details

Step 1 in detail

On SBI Collect

  1. Open SBI Collect (link in Step 1) and click “Educational Institutions”.
  2. Search for “Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur” and select it from the list of educational institutions.
  3. Select “Outreach Activities (OOA)” as the payment category.
  4. Fill in the form with the details given under “What to enter on SBI Collect”, then make the payment.

The same steps, with screenshots, are in the Payment Instructions (PDF) in Step 1.

Important note

Bulk payments

If an institute makes a single bulk payment covering several participants, the person making the payment must enter the initials of every participant covered by that payment in the Remarks field on the SBI Collect payment page.

For example, the name Roger D. Ace should be entered as R.D.A. Enter the initials of the other participants in the same way, separated by commas.

Participants covered by the same bulk payment may upload the same payment receipt with their individual Google Forms. Each participant must still complete their own registration form, and their initials must match those listed in the payment remarks.

If you encounter any difficulty with this process, please contact the organisers at nsm2026iitk@proton.me, with a CC to akaviraj@iitk.ac.in and pushkars21@iitk.ac.in, for assistance.
