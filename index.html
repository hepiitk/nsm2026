01 About the meeting

About the meeting

The National Strings Meeting is a conference for researchers working in string theory, quantum field theory, gravity, mathematical physics and related areas from Indian institutes and universities. It is meant to foster interaction, discussion, the exchange of ideas and collaboration between young researchers and experts.

The 2026 meeting is hosted by the String theory group at the Department of Physics, Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur.

The conference will feature talks by faculty members, postdoctoral researchers, and senior PhD students, along with poster presentations by participants interested in presenting their work. The programme will also include stimulating discussion sessions throughout the conference.
