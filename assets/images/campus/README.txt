These are the photographs supplied by the organisers. They are shown in
index.html, section "IIT KANPUR — PHOTO SLIDESHOW".

To REPLACE a photo: save the new file here under the same name.
To ADD a photo: save it here (e.g. campus-5.jpg) and copy one <li class="slide">
block in index.html. To REMOVE one: delete its <li class="slide"> block.

Larger originals (about 2000 px wide) will look sharper on big screens.
Lower-case file names, no spaces. Record the source/credit of each photo in
README.md (section "Image sources and licences").
